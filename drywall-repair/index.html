Common Questions

What Aurora homeowners want to know

How much does drywall repair cost in Aurora?

?

Small patches usually run a flat minimum. Bigger repairs are priced by the scope of work — square footage, how much texture matching is involved, and whether you need painting after. We give you a clear price before we start. No surprise charges.

How long will the repair take?

?

Most patch work is done in a single visit. Larger jobs with multiple coats of mud may need two visits a day or two apart to let things dry properly. We'll tell you the realistic timeline up front.

Can you match my wall texture?

?

Yes. We work with orange peel, knockdown, smooth finish, and skip trowel textures regularly. If your wall has something unusual, we'll figure out how to match it before we start.

Do I need to paint after the repair?

?

Usually yes. Even a perfectly matched patch will show slightly until it's painted. We can either leave it ready for your painter, or paint it ourselves as part of the job. Your call.

Will the patch be visible after?

?

If done right, no. The trick is good feathering and matching the existing texture. We take time on this part because the patch only looks good once it's painted and matches the wall around it.
