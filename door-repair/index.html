Common Questions

What Aurora homeowners want to know

Can you install a door I bought somewhere else?

?

Yes. Many homeowners pick out a specific door from Home Depot, Lowe's, or a specialty supplier and just need someone to install it. We're happy to do that — bring us the door and we'll hang it right.

How much does it cost to fix a sticking door?

?

Usually a single visit at a basic service rate. If it's a simple hinge adjustment, that's quick. If the door needs to be planed or the frame is shifting, the work takes a bit more time, but we'll tell you up front what the fix is.

Do you do exterior doors?

?

Yes. Exterior doors take a bit more care because they have to seal against the weather, but we handle exterior installs and repairs regularly. Front, back, side entry, all of it.

Can you replace just the frame?

?

Sometimes. If the door itself is fine and just the frame is damaged, we can replace the jamb and trim and re-hang the existing door. Other times the door and frame are sized together and it makes more sense to replace both.

Will the new door match my existing doors?

?

We'll do our best. If you bring us a specific door, we can hang that one. If you're asking us to source it, we'll find a match for what you already have or recommend the closest current product.
