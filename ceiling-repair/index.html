What We Do

What our ceiling repair work includes

Water-Damaged Ceilings

Brown rings, soft drywall, sagging spots from a roof or plumbing leak. We cut out the damage, replace it, retexture, and prime stains so they don't bleed back through.

Ceiling Crack Repair

Hairline cracks from settling, joint cracks where ceiling meets wall, and cracks along seams. Cut back, taped, mudded, and blended in.

Sagging Ceiling Repair

Bowed sections, drywall pulling loose from the joists, soft spots after a leak. Re-secured properly, or cut out and replaced when the panel is too far gone.

Holes & Fixture Patching

Holes from removed light fixtures, ceiling fans, old vents, or accidental damage. Patched flush and textured so the spot disappears.

Popcorn Ceiling Repair

Damaged popcorn texture matched and blended back in so the patch doesn't stand out. Useful when you're not ready to remove the whole ceiling.

Popcorn Ceiling Removal

Scrape off the popcorn, skim coat the ceiling smooth, prime and finish. Most older Aurora homes get a real lift from this. Smooth or new light texture, your call.

Common Questions

What Aurora homeowners want to know

How much does ceiling repair cost in Aurora?

?

Small ceiling patches and crack repairs usually run a flat minimum. Bigger jobs are priced by the scope of work — square footage, whether there is water damage to cut out, whether it's a popcorn texture match or a full popcorn removal, and whether you need painting after. We give you a clear price before we start. No surprise charges.

Can you repair water-damaged ceilings?

?

Yes. Most ceiling jobs we see in Aurora start with a leak. We cut out the soft drywall, make sure the area is dry, replace the damaged section, retexture to match, and prime stains so they don't bleed back through new paint. We recommend the source leak is fixed first so the problem doesn't come back.

Do you remove popcorn ceilings in Aurora?

?

Yes. We scrape the popcorn texture off, skim coat the ceiling smooth, and either leave it as a clean modern smooth finish or apply a new light texture if you'd prefer. Aurora has a lot of older homes with popcorn ceilings, and the upgrade makes a big difference in how a room feels.

How long does ceiling repair take?

?

Small ceiling patches and crack repairs are usually finished in a single visit. Water damage repair and popcorn ceiling work typically need two visits a day or two apart so the mud, primer, and texture can dry properly. We'll tell you the realistic timeline up front.

Will the ceiling patch be visible after?

?

If done right, no. Ceiling repairs can actually be more forgiving than wall patches because most ceilings have texture that hides seams — as long as the texture is matched and the area is primed and painted, the repair disappears into the rest of the ceiling.
