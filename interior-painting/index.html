Common Questions

What Aurora homeowners want to know

How long does it take to paint a room?

?

Most standard bedrooms or offices take a single day for walls. Add trim and ceiling and it's usually a day and a half to two days, depending on prep and how many coats the color needs. We tell you the realistic timeline before we start.

Do you supply the paint or do I?

?

Either way works. We can buy the paint as part of the job or use paint you've already picked out. If we're sourcing it, we use trusted brands with good coverage so you get fewer coats and a better finish.

Can you help me pick a color?

?

We can give opinions, but we're not interior designers. If you're stuck between options, we recommend buying small sample pints from the paint store and trying them on the wall in different light. Then we paint the one you choose.

Do you move furniture and cover floors?

?

Yes. We move furniture to the middle of the room (or out if you'd rather), cover everything with drop cloths, and tape off baseboards and outlets. We treat your floors like they're our floors.

What about touch-ups after?

?

We always leave you with a small amount of leftover paint and labeled cans so you can do future touch-ups yourself. If you'd rather we do them, we're happy to come back.
