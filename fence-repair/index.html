Common Questions

What Aurora homeowners want to know

How long does a wood fence last in Colorado?

?

Cedar or redwood fences typically last 15-25 years with maintenance. The dry climate is actually pretty good for wood, but UV fades it and posts rot at ground level. Replacing one or two failing posts is usually enough to add another decade.

Can you fix just one leaning post or do I need to replace the whole fence?

?

Almost always, a single post or two can be replaced without redoing the whole line. We pull the bad post, dig a fresh hole, and re-anchor with concrete. It looks the same when we're done.

Do you handle HOA requirements?

?

Yes. Many Aurora neighborhoods have specific rules about height, materials, and color. If you can give us a copy of your HOA's fence guidelines, we'll make sure the work matches what they require.

Do you do new fence installs or only repair?

?

Both. If you need a brand new fence put in, we handle layout, post setting, and panel install. If you just need a section fixed, we do that too.

How much does fence repair cost?

?

Single post replacement starts at a flat rate. Panel work and bigger jobs depend on materials and how much fence is involved. We give you a clear price after seeing what needs doing.
