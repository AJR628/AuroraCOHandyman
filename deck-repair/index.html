Common Questions

What Aurora homeowners want to know

How often should I re-stain my deck in Aurora?

?

Most decks in the Front Range need re-staining every 2-3 years because of the intense UV and dry climate. A good stain with UV protection holds up longer. We can assess yours and tell you whether it needs a full refinish or just a touch-up.

Can you repair my existing deck or do I need a new one?

?

Most of the time, repair is the right call. Unless the structure (joists, posts, ledger) is rotted, you can usually fix the cosmetic issues and add years of life. We'll be honest if it's beyond saving.

How much does deck staining cost?

?

It depends on deck size, how much prep work the wood needs, and the type of stain you want. Standard 12x12 deck refinishes start at a fair price for our area. We give you a clear estimate before any work begins.

Do you handle composite decks too?

?

Yes, but the work is different. Composite doesn't need staining — it just needs cleaning, fastener repair, and occasional board replacement. We handle both wood and composite.

How long does staining take?

?

Most decks take 1-2 days depending on size and weather. Stain needs to dry fully before you can walk on it, so we time the work to avoid the next day's forecast.
