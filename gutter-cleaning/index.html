Common Questions

What Aurora homeowners want to know

How often should I clean my gutters in Aurora?

?

Twice a year is the standard — once in late spring after trees finish dropping seed pods, and once in late fall after the leaves come down. If you have big trees right over the gutters, you may need three or four times a year.

Do you handle two-story homes?

?

Yes. We're set up with the right ladders and safety equipment to work on two-story homes. We're careful about the work but more careful about not falling off the roof.

What if my gutters are damaged?

?

We'll point out anything we see while we're up there — sagging sections, loose hangers, leaks, downspout issues — and quote you for the repair separately. Cleaning is one job, repair is another, and we never bundle one into the other without telling you.

Can you install gutter guards?

?

Yes, in cases where they actually help. Under big pine trees they're worth it. On a clean roof with no overhanging trees, they're often unnecessary. We'll give you an honest opinion.

How much does gutter cleaning cost?

?

Depends on home size and how much debris there is. Standard single-story home is one price, two-story is another. We give you a clear quote before any work starts.
