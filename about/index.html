What We Do

The middle ground between weekend DIY and a specialty contractor

There's a category of work that's hard to get done well in Aurora. Too big for you to take on after dinner. Too small to interest a roofing company or a custom builder. That's the work we focus on. Drywall holes that need to disappear. Decks tired from another Front Range summer. Fence posts pulled out of the dirt by a windstorm. A new front door hung properly so it doesn't drag on the carpet. Six core services, all done well, all priced fairly.

We're based in Aurora because Aurora is where we live. We know what Front Range weather does to wood. We know which seasons are best for outdoor work and which are best for inside jobs. We know that "I'll be there at 2" needs to mean 2, not 4:30 with no phone call.
